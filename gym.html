3-Day Aesthetic Split

Build the V-Taper: Mon · Wed · Fri

Wide shoulders, a full chest, a wide back and arms that fill the sleeves. Each session takes about 60–75 minutes. Tick off exercises as you go, log your weights, and use the rest timer between sets.

MonPush
TueRest
WedPull
ThuRest
FriLegs
SatWalk
SunRest

🔥 Warm-up (every session)

  • 5 min light cardio (bike, rower or incline walk)
  • Arm circles, band pull-aparts × 20
  • 2–3 lighter ramp-up sets before your first exercise

📈 How to progress

  • Work in the given rep range. When you hit the top of the range on every set, add 2.5–5 kg next session.
  • Finish most sets 1–2 reps short of failure. Take the last set of isolation moves to failure.
  • Control the lowering phase (2–3 seconds).

🍗 Nutrition for aesthetics

  • Protein: 1.6–2.2 g per kg of bodyweight daily
  • Lean bulk: +200–300 kcal. Cut: −300–500 kcal.
  • Drink 2.5–3 L of water, sleep 7–9 hours

🧘 Rest days

  • Tue/Thu: full rest or 20–30 min light cardio
  • Sat: 8–10k steps, stretching or mobility
  • Sun: full recovery
